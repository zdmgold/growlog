# Screenshot Strategy (draft)

Recommended sequence, one screen per core value proposition rather
than one per app screen:

1. **Home/Garden grid** — "Track all your plants in one place"
2. **Growth timeline slider** — "See real progress, side by side" (this is the single most differentiating visual — lead with it after the hero shot)
3. **Care reminder / NextCareBadge on a plant card** — "Never forget to water again"
4. **Add Plant screen with 6 care-type frequency fields** — "Set it once, per plant"
5. **Settings screen showing "Remove Ads — one-time" and no subscription tiers** — "No subscription. Ever." (directly targets the documented pain point in Planta/PictureThis reviews — this is the differentiator screenshot)
6. **Dark mode variant of the home screen** — quick visual variety, shows polish

Captions should be short (under 8 words), high-contrast, placed above
or below the device frame — not overlaid on top of app content.

Device frames: use real device mockups, not flat screenshots, for
store listing (App Store requires no visible status bar chrome
inconsistencies; Play is more lenient but consistency matters for
conversion).
