Common questions

How does plant identification work?

Tap the camera button and take a photo of a leaf or the whole plant. The first time, GrowLog offers to download the offline model (about 309 MB, over Wi-Fi or mobile data) so it can identify plants on your phone without internet. You see the closest matches and pick the right one, then save it to your garden. You can also use your own AI key instead.

How do I use my own AI key?

Go to Settings → AI, choose a provider, paste your key and tap Connect. AI keys are used for health checks (Doctor), a second opinion on a scan, and chat. Your photo goes to the provider you chose when you use these features.

How do I remove the offline model?

Go to Settings → Offline model and tap it to remove it. You can download it again later.

My reminder didn't notify me

On Android 13 and later, GrowLog asks for notification permission. If you declined, go to your phone's Settings → Apps → GrowLog → Notifications and enable it. Reminders can arrive up to about an hour after the scheduled time so they work without special permissions. Make sure the plant has a care schedule (open the plant and choose Edit schedule).

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings → Export Data to create a backup file you can save or share. Use Settings → Import Data on a new device to restore it.

How do I remove ads?

Go to Settings → Remove Ads. This is a one-time purchase — no subscription, no recurring charge.

Is my data private?

Your plant data stays on your device. Photos identified with the offline model are not uploaded. If you connect your own AI key and use AI features, the photo you send goes to the provider you chose. See our Privacy Policy for details.
