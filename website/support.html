Common questions

My reminder didn't notify me

On Android 13 and later, GrowLog will ask for notification permission the first time you open the app. If you declined this, go to your phone's Settings → Apps → GrowLog → Notifications and enable it.

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings → Export Data. This creates a JSON file with every plant, photo reference, and care log, which you can save or share anywhere you like. Use Settings → Import Data on a new device to restore it.

How do I remove ads?

Go to Settings → Remove Ads. This is a one-time purchase — no subscription, no recurring charge.

Is my data private?

Yes — see our Privacy Policy for full details. All plant data stays on your device.
