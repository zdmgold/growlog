What GrowLog stores

All plant data — names, species, photos, care logs, measurements, and room organization — is stored locally on your device using a local database. GrowLog does not require an account and does not transmit your plant data to any server.

Photos

Photos you add are saved to your device's local storage only. They are never uploaded anywhere by GrowLog.

Advertising

GrowLog displays ads via Google AdMob to support free development, unless you purchase the one-time "Remove Ads" option. AdMob may use an advertising identifier to personalize ads. You will be asked for permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS. You can decline this and still use every feature of GrowLog.

In-App Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely through Apple's App Store or Google Play Billing. GrowLog does not receive or store your payment information.

Backup & Export

You can export your data as a JSON file at any time from Settings. This file is created and shared entirely by you — GrowLog does not automatically upload or transmit it anywhere.

Data deletion

You can delete individual plants, or clear all data at once from Settings. Since all data is local, uninstalling the app also permanently deletes everything.

Children's Privacy

GrowLog does not knowingly collect information from children. The app does not require any account creation or personal information to function.
