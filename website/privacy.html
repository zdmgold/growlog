Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

What GrowLog stores

Your plant data — names, species, photos, care schedules and logs, and room organization — is stored locally on your device in a local database. GrowLog does not require an account, and its developer does not receive your plant data.

Identifying plants (offline model)

GrowLog can identify plants on your phone with an offline model. When you use it, the photo is processed on your device and is not uploaded anywhere. It works without an internet connection once the model has been downloaded.

Downloading the offline model

The offline model (about 309 MB) is downloaded once, only when you choose to, from a public GitHub repository. Like any download, GitHub can see standard request information such as your IP address. The download does not include your photos or plant data. You can remove the model at any time in Settings.

Optional AI features (your own key)

If you connect your own AI provider key (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, xAI, Mistral, Groq, OpenRouter or Cerebras), you can use features such as plant health checks, a second opinion, and chat. Your key is stored encrypted on your device and is sent only to the provider you chose. When you use these features, the photo or message you send goes to that provider and is handled under that provider's terms and privacy policy — some providers may use data from free tiers to improve their products, so check theirs. GrowLog's developer does not receive these photos, messages or your key. These features are used only when you start them.

Advertising

GrowLog displays ads through Google AdMob to support free development, unless you purchase the one-time “Remove Ads” option. AdMob may use an advertising identifier. On Android you can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your device's Settings. On iOS, GrowLog does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so ads there are not personalized with your advertising identifier. You can use every feature of GrowLog whether or not you see ads.

In-App Purchases

The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is processed entirely through Apple's App Store or Google Play Billing. GrowLog does not receive or store your payment information.

Backup & Export

You can export your data as a file at any time from Settings. The file is created and shared entirely by you — GrowLog does not automatically upload or transmit it anywhere.

Data deletion

You can delete individual plants or clear all data at once from Settings, and you can remove the offline model in Settings. Because your data is stored on your device, uninstalling the app also permanently deletes it.

Children's Privacy

GrowLog does not knowingly collect information from children. The app does not require any account or personal information to function.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change with it.
