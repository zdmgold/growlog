Know what it is.

GrowLog identifies your plants right on your phone — no internet and no account needed — and helps you keep them alive with care reminders. Free to use, with an optional one-time ad removal.

Identify offline

Download the on-device model once (about 309 MB) and identify plants without internet. Your photos stay on your phone.

Care Reminders

Water, fertilize, mist, repot, prune, or treat — set a schedule per plant and get reminded before you forget.

Doctor, with your own AI key

Optionally connect your own AI provider for health checks and a second opinion. Photos go to the provider you choose, only when you ask.

Rooms & Wishlist

Organize plants by room, and keep a wishlist of plants you're planning to bring home next.

Your Data, Your Device

Your garden is stored locally. Export a backup any time — no account required, ever.

11 Languages & Dark Mode

Right-to-left support for Arabic and Hebrew, and a light or dark theme that follows your phone.

Why free, actually free?

Most plant apps lock basics behind a subscription. GrowLog doesn't. The only purchase is a one-time, optional ad removal — identifying and caring for your plants is never paywalled.
